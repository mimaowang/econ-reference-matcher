# Direct Citation Fitness Rubric

Direct citation fitness asks whether a paper can honestly be cited immediately after the target passage.

## Scoring Questions

1. Which exact claim in the passage does the paper support?
2. Is the paper's evidence about the same relationship, mechanism, definition, or contribution claim?
3. Does the evidence support the passage as written, or only a weaker version?
4. Would the citation mislead a reader if placed after the target sentence?
5. Is the paper better classified as theory support, literature dialogue, pending full text, or rejected?

## Categories

- `Direct Support`: supports a specific target claim with traceable evidence.
- `Theory Support`: supports the mechanism or theoretical foundation, but not the exact empirical claim.
- `Literature Dialogue`: helps position contribution, contrast, consistency, or prior work.
- `Strong Candidate Pending Full Text`: promising, but evidence is not yet traceable.
- `Topic Adjacent / Rejected`: shares topic words but cannot support the claim.

## High-Quality Output

A high-quality output:

- maps each final paper to a claim ID;
- gives a short verifiable excerpt or marks the candidate pending;
- explains how the evidence supports the claim;
- rejects tempting decoys;
- does not use journal prestige or topical similarity as a substitute for support.
