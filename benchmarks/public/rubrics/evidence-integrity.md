# Evidence Integrity Rubric

Evidence integrity measures whether the output is traceable and honest.

## Requirements

- Use short excerpts only.
- Provide location: abstract, page, section, DOI page, publisher URL, PDF URL, or user-provided excerpt.
- Do not invent article text, DOI, page numbers, journal ranking, SSCI membership, or findings.
- Mark inaccessible but promising papers as `Strong Candidate Pending Full Text`.
- State when a claim is unsupported under the available evidence.

## Critical Failures

The grader should apply a critical cap when the output:

- fabricates a quote or page number;
- fabricates DOI, SSCI, JCR, ABS/AJG, FT50, or UTD status;
- labels a forbidden decoy as `Direct Support`;
- copies private gold canary content into the answer.
