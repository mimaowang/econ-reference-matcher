# Manuscript Context

This manuscript studies whether digital marketplace participation changes the growth path of small manufacturing firms. The theory section argues that online marketplaces reduce market frictions by widening demand access and reducing search costs between firms and potential buyers.

The contribution is not simply to show that digital platforms correlate with better firm performance. The paper aims to isolate a demand-access mechanism: small firms can reach buyers outside their local networks and lower customer acquisition frictions after joining a platform.
