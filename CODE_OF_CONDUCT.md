# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to make this project welcoming, respectful, and useful for researchers, students, maintainers, and contributors across institutions and countries.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and implementation choices rather than people.
- Respect copyright, database licensing, and privacy constraints.
- Avoid sharing private manuscripts, proprietary journal lists, or sealed benchmark answers in public channels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private user data, manuscripts, credentials, or sealed evaluation materials.
- Misrepresenting benchmark results, journal status, or citation evidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove issues, pull requests, comments, and contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to blocking from the project.
